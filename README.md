# Holo Client

**A cleaner Minecraft 1.8.9 client, built around customization, smooth visuals, and a better user experience.**

Holo Client is a community focused Minecraft 1.8.9 client project created to explore what a polished, customizable, and modern free client can look and feel like.

Our goal is simple: bring together a clean interface, configurable features, smooth visual effects, and a client experience that feels thoughtfully designed, without making the project inaccessible to people who do not want to pay for a client.

## ✨ Why Holo?

Many free clients focus primarily on functionality. Holo aims to put just as much attention into **how the client feels to use**.

We want a client that looks clean, feels responsive, gives users control over their experience, and continues to improve through development and community feedback.

### What makes Holo different?

1. **🎨 A modern visual direction**

   A clean, minimal aesthetic inspired by the polished feel of premium Minecraft clients.

2. **⚡ Smooth animations**

   A focus on fluid transitions and subtle effects instead of unnecessary visual clutter.

3. **🛠️ Customization first**

   Options that let users shape their client experience around their own preferences.

4. **🖥️ A personalized HUD**

   A configurable interface designed to keep useful information accessible without overcrowding the screen.

5. **🔓 Free and community focused**

   A project intended to be accessible, with room for feedback, contributions, and improvements.

6. **🚀 Continuous improvement**

   A development approach focused on refining existing features, fixing issues, and improving overall quality.

## 🎯 Our Philosophy

Holo is not about adding as many features as possible just to make a longer feature list.

It is about making the overall experience better.

We believe a good client should be visually consistent, responsive, configurable, and enjoyable to use. Small details such as spacing, animation timing, color choices, readable text, and interface consistency matter just as much as the features themselves.

## 🆚 Why choose Holo over other free clients?

Holo aims to offer an alternative for players who want more than a basic interface or a collection of modules.

| Focus | Holo's goal |
|---|---|
| Visual design | A consistent, modern look |
| User experience | Smooth, responsive interactions |
| Customization | More control over appearance and settings |
| Development | Ongoing refinement and bug fixing |
| Accessibility | A free project that people can explore and contribute to |

We do not claim that every other client is worse. Different clients serve different needs. Holo's goal is to stand out through its combination of visual polish, customization, and community driven development.

## 🧩 Development Status

Holo Client is a work in progress. Features, visuals, and internal systems may change as development continues.

Some ideas are still being refined, so the feature set and stability may vary between versions.

## ✨ Features

Holo Client focuses on customization, visual polish, and a comfortable Minecraft 1.8.9 experience.

### 🎨 Customization

Personalize your experience with configurable visual settings, accent colors, and interface options.

### 🖥️ Multiple ClickGUI Styles

Choose from several available interface styles, including Normal, Raven B3, Raven B4, Cheadle, Modern, and Rise.

### 🌈 Custom Colors

Adjust the interface accent color to match your personal style.

### 🌫️ Visual Effects

Options for shadows, glass effects, and rounded corners help create a cleaner and more modern interface.

### 📊 Custom HUD

Configure HUD elements and visual settings to keep your in game information organized and readable.

### ⚙️ Configurable Settings

Customize supported features through their available settings to make the client feel more personal.

### 💎 Smooth and Modern Design

Holo aims to deliver a polished experience through consistent spacing, clean visuals, and smooth animations as development progresses.

### 🆓 Free to Use

Holo is designed as a free client project for players who want customization and a modern visual direction without a paid entry requirement.

### 🔧 Active Development

Holo continues to evolve as features are improved, visual details are refined, and bugs are fixed.
